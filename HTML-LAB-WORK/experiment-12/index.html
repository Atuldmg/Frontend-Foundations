Ancient Pataliputra • Capital of Bihar

PATNA (PATALIPUTRA)

One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited capitals, founded in 490 BCE on the banks of the sacred River Ganga — the historic seat of the Mauryan and Gupta Empires, home to Aryabhata, Chanakya, and Guru Gobind Singh Ji.

📍 Bihar, India ⏳ >2,500 Years Heritage 🌊 River Ganga & Gandak 🏛️ Seat of Ashoka the Great

The Iconic Golghar & Ganga Riverfront

Built in 1786, the monumental stupa-shaped granary of Golghar stands overlooking the majestic Ganges, offering a 360-degree panoramic vista across the city of Patna through its twin spiral stairways of 145 steps each.

✨ Cradle of Indian Democracy, Mathematics & Statecraft

🏛️ Historical Glory & Imperial Legacy

Patna, historically known as Pataliputra and Kusumpura, served as the epicenter of power for the greatest empires of ancient India, including the Nanda Empire, Mauryan Empire, and Gupta Empire. Megasthenes, the Greek ambassador to the court of Chandragupta Maurya, described Pataliputra as one of the grandest cities in the ancient civilized world.

The city is the birthplace of intellectual giants: Aryabhata (who pioneered the concept of zero and astronomical calculations at Taregna near Patna), Chanakya (author of the Arthashastra), and Emperor Ashoka the Great, who spread the principles of Dhamma across the Asian continent.

📍 Key Landmarks & Heritage Sites

☬

Takht Sri Patna Sahib

One of the Five Holy Takhts of Sikhism, marking the revered birthplace of the tenth Sikh Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji, built in white marble on the bank of the Ganges.

Sacred Spiritual Takht
🏺

Bihar Museum & Kumhrar

A state-of-the-art international museum preserving the world-famous Didarganj Yakshi, Mauryan pillar relics at Kumhrar, and 3,000-year-old bronze sculptures.

World Archaeological Treasury
🛕

Mahavir Mandir & Buddha Smriti Park

One of the most visited Hanuman temples in North India paired with the serene 22-acre Buddha Smriti Park housing sacred Buddhist relics inaugurated by the Dalai Lama.

Cultural & Peace Center

🍲 Iconic Bihari Delicacies

  • Litti Chokha: Roasted whole-wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced sattu (roasted gram), dipped in pure desi ghee, paired with baingan-aloo chokha.
  • Silao Khaja: Crispy, multi-layered delicacy recognized with a prestigious Geographical Indication (GI Tag).
  • Naivedyam & Anarsa: Ghee-fried rice flour sweets coated with sesame seeds, prepared during traditional festivals.
  • Tilkut & Thekua: Famous traditional festive sweets prepared with jaggery, whole wheat, and fennel seeds.

🎨 Art, Tikuli & Madhubani Heritage

Patna is the cultural center for Tikuli Art (an 800-year-old indigenous craft created on glazed hardboard with gold enamel) and the world-renowned Madhubani (Mithila) Paintings depicting nature and mythology.

🏷️ Geographical Heritage: Renowned for Bhagalpuri Tussar Silk, Sikki Grass Craft, and Madhubani Folk Art.
